# Prosperya site

The marketing site for Prosperya, a Paris enterprise-systems consultancy. These terms name the recurring parts of its pages and the visual language that ties them together.

## Language

**Hero**:
The first screen of a page: one claim, one lead, its actions and one visual.
_Avoid_: Banner, header, first div

**Hub**:
Prosperya's signature motif: a central core with labelled systems connected around it.
_Avoid_: Network graphic, radial diagram, orchestration diagram

**Close**:
The final section of a page, which offers the reader their next step.
_Avoid_: CTA band, footer CTA, final CTA

**Wave**:
The flowing-lines motif used as a background for a Close and on the Approach page.
_Avoid_: Signal wave, ribbon

**Pinned story**:
A section that holds in place while scrolling advances its steps.
_Avoid_: Scrolljack, sticky scene, scroll story

**Marquee**:
The strip of client marks that drifts continuously across the page.
_Avoid_: Logo wall, ticker, carousel

## Relationships

- A page has exactly one **Hero** and one **Close**
- A page shows the **Hub** at most once
- A page has at most one **Pinned story**, and never in its **Hero**
- The **Marquee** appears on the Home page only
